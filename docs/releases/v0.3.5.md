# ResampleOS 0.3.5

A sample browser inside the app, MIDI files in and out, stem export and track freezing. These are the first parts of the 0.4 plan, released early. Projects from 0.3.0 open as they were.

> **Pre-release.** Developed and tested on Windows 11 only. Expect rough edges, and save often.

## New

### The sample browser

**Samples** in the top bar (or `F4`) opens a browser for your own sample folders down the left of the screen, beside the pads or the song timeline.

- **Add folder…** adds a folder of samples, as many as you like. They are remembered with the settings and listed in the background, so even a large library is searched without waiting on the disk.
- Browse a folder's subfolders, or **search** every folder at once by name.
- **★ Favourites**, and a **Recent** list of the files you used lately.
- **Audition:** click a file to hear it, at its own level, and step through the list with the arrow keys. Auditions play after the master, so they are never processed, captured by skip-back or resampled.
- **Drag** a file onto a pad, or onto the song timeline (onto a track, or onto empty space for a new track). **Enter** or a double-click puts it on the selected pad, or on the timeline at the insert marker in the song view; **Shift+Enter** puts it on the next empty pad.

### MIDI files

- **File > Import MIDI file…**, or drag a `.mid` file in from Explorer or the sample browser. On the pads, each part becomes a pad's pattern (up to sixteen bars) on the pad dropped on and those after it; empty pads become MIDI pads. On the timeline, each part becomes a clip of notes of its own, as long as the part runs.
- **Export selected pad as MIDI…** writes a pad's pattern; **Export song as MIDI…** writes every track's notes over the whole song (loops written out where their clips play), a track per pad, drum pads on channel 10.
- Format 0 and 1 files at any resolution are read; SMPTE-timed files are not.

### Stems and freeze

- **Stems** beside **Export song…**: one WAV per track (each pad that plays in the song, through its bus and effects) or per bus, into a folder you choose. Stems leave out the master's effects, so together they make the mix before the master.
- **Freeze** (right-click a track's header) renders the track into audio and switches its instrument plugin off, to spare the processor. **Unfreeze** puts the clips and the plugin back. Undo covers both.

## Improved

- The arrangement's track headers fit their **R**, **M** and **S** buttons, and long names are shortened with "…" (hover for the whole name). Both changes were also made late to the 0.3.0 download.
- The top bar's transport area is a little wider, to make room for **Samples**.

## Install

**From the download:** unzip `ResampleOS-0.3.5-windows-x64.zip` and run `resampleos.exe`. There is no installer and nothing is written outside the folder except settings in `%APPDATA%\ResampleOS`. Windows may show a SmartScreen warning because the program is not signed: choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**. If it reports a missing `VCRUNTIME140.dll`, install the [Microsoft Visual C++ Redistributable](https://learn.microsoft.com/cpp/windows/latest-supported-vc-redist).

**Upgrading from 0.3.0:** replace the folder. Settings and projects carry over. A project saved in 0.3.5 opens in 0.3.0, but a frozen track opens there as its frozen audio only; unfreeze tracks before opening a project in an older version.

**From source:** you need [Rust](https://rustup.rs) 1.88 or newer and the MSVC build tools.

```bash
git clone https://github.com/DamonianoStudios/ResampleOS
cd ResampleOS
cargo run --release -- showcase/built-in-boom-bap
```

The [user guide](https://github.com/DamonianoStudios/ResampleOS/blob/v0.3.5/docs/USER_GUIDE.md) has a new section on the sample browser, and the [roadmap](https://github.com/DamonianoStudios/ResampleOS/blob/v0.3.5/docs/ROADMAP.md) shows what is left for 0.4.

## Known limitations

- The sample browser lists by file name; analysis of tempo, key and one-shot or loop, filters on them, and auditioning loops at the project tempo come later.
- MIDI import and export carry notes only: no controllers, pitch bend or tempo changes. Exported MIDI was checked by reading it back, not yet in another DAW.
- No sends, return tracks or extra buses yet.
- A crash reported while editing steps on a looping vocal pad could not be reproduced; the only crash on record from that session was inside a Waves plugin. If it happens to you, please report which plugins were loaded.
- Everything listed for 0.3.0 still applies: Windows only, VST3 and CLAP only, one tempo in 4/4, no plugin delay compensation, no ASIO, plugins not crash-isolated.
