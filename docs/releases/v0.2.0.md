# ResampleOS 0.2.0

Songs, not just loops. The new arrangement view turns loops into a song as long as you like, and exports it to WAV. Also new: CLAP plugin hosting, automation of drum knobs and plugin parameters with curved lanes, three ways to warp audio, sharper pitch detection, analyzer slots, and a run of fixes to the limitations listed for 0.1.2. Projects from 0.1.2 open as they were.

![The arrangement view](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/DamonianoStudios/ResampleOS/v0.2.0/docs/images/arrangement.png)

> **Pre-release.** Developed and tested on Windows 11 only. Expect rough edges, and save often.

## New

### The arrangement

- **A song timeline beside the pads.** **Song** in the top bar (or `F3`) swaps the pad matrix and inspector for a timeline as long as the song needs. Every pad that plays in a loop gets a track.
- **Clips play loops.** A clip plays one of the pad's loops, round and round to fill it, so editing a loop changes every clip that plays it. **Make unique** copies the loop when one clip should go its own way.
- **From jam to song in one click.** **Chain to song** writes the chain out as a song: a section per loop in the new **LOOPS** lane, a clip per pad. **Put loop** drops any loop in at the insert marker.
- **Editing:** move, trim either end (a clip can start part-way into its loop), duplicate (`Ctrl+D`), split at the marker (`Ctrl+E`), delete, switch a clip to another loop. Clips dropped on others cut them back, never overlap. Sections move and duplicate with their clips.
- **Loop automation follows the loop.** A section plays its loop's automation, so a sweep drawn in a loop happens wherever that loop is in the song.
- **Playback:** play from the insert marker, a loop brace to go round a stretch, follow, zoom and scroll. Launch a loop over the song and it takes over at once; **Back to arrangement** picks the song up where it would have been.
- **Export song.** Renders the song (or the loop brace) to WAV in 24-bit, 16-bit dithered or 32-bit float, effect tails included, through the same engine as playback, faster than real time.
- Undo covers the arrangement, and it is saved with the project. A project with a song opens on it.

### Plugins

- **CLAP plugins** are hosted alongside VST3: instruments and effects, their own editor windows, saved state, parameters. They are marked **CLAP** in the browser. Tested with Vital and MIDISketch.
- **Analyzer slots.** **Analyzers** under the master's effect slots opens four slots for metering plugins such as SPAN, Insight or Lucent. Each listens to a bus after its fader; what it puts out is not heard, so it can't change the mix or use up an effect slot.

### Automation

- **Drum knobs and plugin parameters** can be automated, in instruments and bus effects alike. Move a control in a plugin's own window and the Automation tab offers to automate it (**Automate …, last touched**), or find it under **Add lane** with a filter. With **Rec** on, moves in a plugin's window are recorded too.
- **Curves.** Drag the small ring between two points to bend the line between them; double-click it to straighten it.
- While playing, a thin amber bar under an automated control shows where automation has it.

### Warping and pitch

- **Three ways to warp**, in the Warp panel and the harmonizer:
  - **Elastic** (the new default) is a phase vocoder: pitch and length change independently and smoothly, chords and voices stay steady, and hits keep their attack.
  - **Beats** is the 0.1 method, improved: it splices between hits, for drums.
  - **Varispeed** changes the speed, as tape does: faster is higher and shorter, with no artefacts at all.
- **Keep formants** (Elastic) holds the vowel and body of a sound in place while its pitch moves, so a shifted voice doesn't sound chipmunked or giant. Vocal doubles pitched up or down now keep the singer's formants.
- **Pitch detection** reads a melody by the note it dwells on, and holds up under vibrato, breath noise and a missing fundamental.
- **Pitch Lock** tracks high voices to within a fraction of a cent; it was up to 9 cents out above 600 Hz.

### Everything else

- **File > New project** (`Ctrl+N`), asking first if there are unsaved changes.
- **Chord velocities.** In the piano roll's velocity lane, dragging a stem moves the whole stack at that position, keeping the chord's voicing, or only the selected notes in it. Each note has its own dot on the lane.
- **Randomize vel** moves velocities by a random amount up to a set range, on the selected notes or all of them.
- **Measure** in the Vocal tab measures the input latency for you: a few chirps go out and come back through a loopback cable or a microphone near a speaker.
- **Pad lights** (Preferences > MIDI Controllers, off by default) light a controller's pads to match the screen.

## Improved

- **Audio settings apply in place.** A new output device, buffer size or skip-back length restarts the audio when you press Save, project untouched. A new sample rate still needs a restart, and **Save and restart** now brings the project back as it was, saved or not.
- **Projects keep every take** of every pad, not only the active one.
- **Silent plugin effects no longer mute their bus.** An effect that puts out nothing for a few seconds while being fed a clear signal is passed by, and its slot says **silent**.
- **Resampling is band-limited** everywhere (varispeed, pitch bakes, loading files at another sample rate): no aliasing when reading faster.
- **The piano roll's toolbar** stays on one row and scrolls sideways in a small window, rather than taking room from the grid.
- **Stopping the transport** now puts automated controls back where they were set by hand, as the user guide always said.

## Install

**From the download:** unzip `ResampleOS-0.2.0-windows-x64.zip` and run `resampleos.exe`. There is no installer and nothing is written outside the folder except settings in `%APPDATA%\ResampleOS`. Windows may show a SmartScreen warning because the program is not signed: choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**. If it reports a missing `VCRUNTIME140.dll`, install the [Microsoft Visual C++ Redistributable](https://learn.microsoft.com/cpp/windows/latest-supported-vc-redist).

**Upgrading from 0.1.2:** replace the folder. Settings and projects carry over. The plugin browser rescans by itself the first time it opens, to find your CLAP plugins. A project saved in 0.2.0 opens in 0.1.2 without its arrangement, analyzers and earlier takes, and not at all if it automates a drum knob or a plugin parameter.

**From source:** you need [Rust](https://rustup.rs) 1.88 or newer and the MSVC build tools.

```bash
git clone https://github.com/DamonianoStudios/ResampleOS
cd ResampleOS
cargo run --release -- showcase/built-in-boom-bap
```

The demo opens on its song; press **Space**. **F1** shows the shortcuts. The [user guide](https://github.com/DamonianoStudios/ResampleOS/blob/v0.2.0/docs/USER_GUIDE.md) has a new section on the arrangement, and the [roadmap](https://github.com/DamonianoStudios/ResampleOS/blob/v0.2.0/docs/ROADMAP.md) says what comes next.

## Known limitations

- Windows only for now. CLAP editors open on Windows only.
- VST3 and CLAP only: no VST2 or AU.
- The arrangement plays patterns. Audio, vocal and step pads play in it through their patterns, as they do in loops; recording straight onto the timeline, audio clips with their own region and fades, and song-length automation lanes come in 0.3.
- A clip always plays a loop; making one unique needs a free loop, and a project holds sixteen.
- Vocal takes are recorded in loops, not on the song timeline.
- The song is in 4/4 at one tempo.
- Hosted plugins are not crash-isolated once loaded; only scanning is.
- Plugins are lent to a song export while it runs, so playback waits for it.
- No plugin delay compensation: a plugin with lookahead pulls its bus slightly late.
- Monitoring through the app is only as fast as shared-mode Windows audio allows; there is no ASIO yet.
- Input latency is measured with a loopback (a cable, or a microphone near a speaker).
- Pitch Lock is for one dry voice. Vocal recording and pitch correction were tested with imported audio and test signals, not a live microphone.
- The drum synth's factory sounds and the demo track were balanced by measured level, not tuned by ear. The new warp methods, the arrangement and the export were checked by measurement and on screen, and have not been listened to critically.
- Pad lights follow the common convention (a pad's own note); controllers that need their own lighting messages stay dark.
