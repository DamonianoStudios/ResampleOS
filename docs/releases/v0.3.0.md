# ResampleOS 0.3.0

The arrangement, finished. Audio on the timeline, recording onto it, and a memory of everything you play, so the best part of a jam can be put into the song after the fact. Also new: song-length automation, markers, inserting and deleting time, consolidate and bake, crossfades, audio that follows the tempo, and fixes for CLAP plugins from LX Audiolabs and others built the same way. Projects from 0.2 open as they were.

![The arrangement](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/DamonianoStudios/ResampleOS/v0.3.0/docs/images/arrangement.png)

> **Pre-release.** Developed and tested on Windows 11 only. Expect rough edges, and save often.

## New

### Audio on the timeline

- **Audio clips.** Drop audio files on a track (or empty space, for a new track), use **Import audio…**, or right-click a track to put the pad's own sample there. Audio clips play locked to the song clock, starting in the right place wherever playback starts and going round the loop brace. They move, trim, split, duplicate and delete like any clip.
- **Gain and fades** per clip, drawn on it; every edge gets a few milliseconds of fade, so cuts never click.
- **Crossfades** between audio clips that meet, with equal-power curves.
- **Follow the tempo.** Recordings are stretched to the project tempo when it changes (Elastic, Beats or Varispeed), in the background; set the tempo a recording was made at, or let it play at its own speed.

### Recording onto the song

- **Arm a track** with **R** on its header and turn on **● Rec** (`F9`). Armed vocal tracks, and audio tracks with no sample, record the input; other armed tracks record the notes played on them. What was recorded is laid down where it was played when you stop.
- **Takes round the loop brace.** Each pass of audio is kept; the last whole pass plays, and **Takes** in the clip's menu switches between them. Notes from every pass are merged.
- **Recording the arrangement from the loops.** With Rec on, loops launched from the pads, perform mode or a controller are written in as sections where they played.
- **New recording track** makes an empty armed track for a vocal or an instrument.

### Capture performance

Everything played while the transport runs is remembered for ten minutes, whether Rec was on or not: loops launched, pads hit, controls moved. **Capture** puts the last 4 to 64 bars of it on the timeline where it happened: loops as sections, hits as clips, control moves as song automation.

### Editing the song

- **Clips with notes of their own**, from recording or capture, play without using one of the sixteen loops. **Open as a loop** turns them into a loop to edit in the piano roll.
- **Song automation.** **Automation** opens song-length lanes under the timeline, lined up with it, that play over the sections' loop automation.
- **Markers** along the ruler: click to go there, drag to move, right-click to rename.
- **Insert and delete time** from the ruler's right-click menu: a bar, or the stretch under the loop brace. Clips, sections, markers and song automation move with it.
- **Section names and colours**: a "Chorus 2" playing the Chorus loop, in a colour of its own.
- **Consolidate** (`Ctrl+J`) renders the selected clips of a track into one audio clip in their place. **Bake to a new track** renders clips from any tracks through their buses and effects onto a new track.
- **Box select** by dragging across empty timeline, and **Ctrl+A** to select the whole song, so `Ctrl+A` then `Del` clears it.
- **Snap** on or off in the toolbar, a grid down to 1/32, and `Alt` to place anything off the grid. **Split here** in a clip's menu splits where you clicked.

## Fixed

- **Lucent showed a blank window**, as could LX Audiolabs' other CLAP plugins, which are built the same way. Their editors draw from a Windows timer that never got through while ResampleOS redraws; timer messages are now delivered every frame. Editors are also told the display scale, so they open at the right size on high-DPI screens.
- **Removing such a plugin, or starting a new project with one loaded, closed ResampleOS.** The plugin's file was unloaded while work it had started was still running. Plugin files now stay loaded until the app quits.

## Install

**From the download:** unzip `ResampleOS-0.3.0-windows-x64.zip` and run `resampleos.exe`. There is no installer and nothing is written outside the folder except settings in `%APPDATA%\ResampleOS`. Windows may show a SmartScreen warning because the program is not signed: choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**. If it reports a missing `VCRUNTIME140.dll`, install the [Microsoft Visual C++ Redistributable](https://learn.microsoft.com/cpp/windows/latest-supported-vc-redist).

**Upgrading from 0.2:** replace the folder. Settings and projects carry over. A project saved in 0.3.0 opens in 0.2.0, but without its markers, song automation, section names and colours, and its audio clips and clips with notes of their own play loop 1's pattern there instead.

**From source:** you need [Rust](https://rustup.rs) 1.88 or newer and the MSVC build tools.

```bash
git clone https://github.com/DamonianoStudios/ResampleOS
cd ResampleOS
cargo run --release -- showcase/built-in-boom-bap
```

The demo opens on its song; press **Space**. **F1** shows the shortcuts. The [user guide](https://github.com/DamonianoStudios/ResampleOS/blob/v0.3.0/docs/USER_GUIDE.md) covers recording, capture and audio clips in its arrangement section, and the [roadmap](https://github.com/DamonianoStudios/ResampleOS/blob/v0.3.0/docs/ROADMAP.md) has what comes next, starting with a built-in sample browser.

## Known limitations

- Windows only for now. CLAP editors open on Windows only.
- VST3 and CLAP only: no VST2 or AU.
- One tempo and 4/4 for the whole song.
- Notes recorded onto the timeline are placed where they were played, within a few milliseconds; there is no record quantize yet (quantize them after **Open as a loop**).
- Recording replaces what was on an armed track over the stretch recorded; notes are merged across brace passes, audio is kept as takes.
- Audio recording onto the timeline was checked with its parts (the timing of each pass, latency compensation, takes) and on screen for notes, loops and capture, but not yet with a live microphone.
- Stretching to a new tempo is done ahead of time: a long recording plays at its old speed for a few seconds after a tempo change, until its stretch is ready.
- Hosted plugins are not crash-isolated once loaded; only scanning is. Plugin files stay loaded until ResampleOS quits.
- Plugins are lent to a render (export, consolidate, bake) while it runs, so playback waits for it.
- No plugin delay compensation; no ASIO yet.
- The new features were checked by measurement and on screen and have not been listened to critically.
